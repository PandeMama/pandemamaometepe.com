Our coffee

One origin.
Roasted here.

Our beans come from the highest mountains of Nueva Segovia in northern Nicaragua. We hand-select every bean and roast in small batches several times a week, right here on the island, so every cup is fresh.

  • 01

    Single origin, Nueva Segovia

    High-altitude beans from northern Nicaragua.

  • 02

    Small-batch, roasted in house

    We roast several times a week. Never stale.

  • 03

    Island milk, pasteurized by us

    Our lattes use local raw milk we slow-pasteurize ourselves.

  • 04

    A grinder that measures every dose

    Our new precision grinder weighs out every shot, and our red espresso machine pulls it. Same perfect cup, every time.

Made from scratch
